Loan solutions for practical growth

Flexible financing pathways designed to support households, enterprise, education, and productive activity.

💼

Business Loan

Working capital and growth support for stock, operations, equipment, or business expansion.

🎓

School Fees Loan

Planned education support for families and guardians meeting important school fee obligations.

🌱

Agri-Business Loan

Finance for productive agricultural activity, including inputs, trading, livestock, and value addition.

🛡️

Secured Loan

Financing supported by acceptable security and assessed according to the applicant's capacity and purpose.

🤝

Unsecured Loan

Flexible personal or business support that may be assessed using income, business performance, and repayment capacity.

💰

Salary Loan

Short-term support for eligible salaried customers, subject to income verification and agreed repayment terms.

🚙

Motor Vehicle Loan

Support for purchasing or improving a productive vehicle for personal, commercial, or business use.

🏍️

Boda-Boda Loan

Practical financing support for eligible riders seeking a motorcycle for income-generating transport work.

🏠

Mortgage Loan

Long-term property financing support for eligible applicants, subject to valuation, documentation, and approval.

🔧

Asset Finance

Support for acquiring productive equipment, tools, machinery, or other assets that strengthen operations.

⚡

Emergency Loan

Short-term assistance for urgent personal needs, assessed carefully against affordability and repayment capacity.

👥

Group Loan

Structured financing for organized groups, associations, and savings teams with clear objectives and records.

All products are subject to eligibility checks, affordability assessment, documentation, approval, applicable charges, and agreed repayment terms.

Our mission is simple: help businesses grow with dependable people, and help job seekers discover meaningful opportunities with confidence.
